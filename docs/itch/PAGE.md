## Details (the description)

Paste into the description box as formatted text, or switch the editor to HTML
and paste it rendered. Under 600 words, on purpose.

> ## Havenbrook has fallen asleep, and it won't wake up.
>
> You're found on the road at the end of a long night and carted into a town where people have been falling asleep one by one and not waking. With a Dreamcatcher in hand, you step into the sleepers' dreams to find out why, and bring them back.
>
> **Solace/Reverie: Quest into the Dreamscape** is a top-down pixel-art action RPG set in the Hollowmarch: forests, swamps, mines, crypts, a frozen north and a palace of brimstone, with the Reverie, a dream world different every night, beneath it all. Play alone, online with up to three friends, or with a second player on the couch.
>
> ### What you do
>
> Choose a calling: the **Lucid Knight** (heavy melee), the **Shade Ranger** (bow and blade), the **Dreamweaver** (ranged magic) or the **Lantern Warden**, whose lit lantern guards like a shield and heals those beside them. Then take the road out of town.
>
> Fights are real time: chained blows, charged heavy swings, parries, blocks and rolls, bows that loose real arrows, and six schools of magic, from fire to an ancient magic taught at the College. Bosses glow before their big moves, so learn to step out of the light.
>
> Between fights you gather and make: mine, chop, fish and forage, then smith, craft, tan, weave, brew, cook and enchant, every trade climbing to 99. Each sleeper you wake opens their trade to you again: the smith's forge, the inn's kitchen, the tanner's yard.
>
> ### What pushes back
>
> The wilds change after dark: things come out at night that don't live there by day, a step stronger than their neighbours. A level in red is still past you. Fall, and you wake in Havenbrook with everything you carried, and a long walk back.
>
> ### What you keep
>
> Your character keeps everything: skills, gear in twelve tiers from wood to enchanted, a skill tree for each calling, boons from the bosses you beat and their totems for your house. At night you can sleep, or dream: the Reverie goes deeper a level at a time, and nothing in a dream can kill you.
>
> ### What's in it
>
> - The prologue and **Acts I and II**, across more than a hundred maps
> - 48 spells, seventeen named beasts on the Guild's ledger, nine questlines and dozens of side quests
> - Co-op for up to four online, or two at one PC in split screen
> - Keyboard or controller, every control rebindable
>
> ### The download
>
> **Windows, 64-bit, about 51 MB.** Unzip anywhere and run `DreamQuest.exe` (the game's working name). Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed: **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once. Saves live in your Windows profile, so updating or deleting the game never touches them. Online co-op runs over [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) (free); `PLAY.txt` in the zip has the controls and how to host.
>
> ### How it was made
>
> Solace/Reverie is made by one person working with an AI, Anthropic's Claude. The story comes from scripts I wrote; the design and the testing are mine. The AI wrote the code, much of the text beyond the main story, and the music, composed in code the game plays live. No image generator drew the art: every sprite is a 3D model built from simple shapes by scripts the AI wrote, rendered in Blender and brought down to pixel art. Every build passes the game's own self-test, over 84,000 checks.
>
> ### What would help
>
> This is early access, and the next version is shaped by what you tell me. Most of all: **where did you get stuck or lost**, did the prologue hold you or did you want to be playing sooner, and **which fight felt unfair**? Leave a comment.
